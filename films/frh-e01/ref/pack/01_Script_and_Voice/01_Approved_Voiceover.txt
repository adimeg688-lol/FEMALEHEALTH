Do you ever look in the mirror and wonder…
Where did I go?

Your memory. Your confidence. Your desire.
Even your body feels unfamiliar.

Menopause could be part of the reason.
Hormone replacement therapy, or HRT, can improve sleep, ease hot flashes, vaginal dryness and menopause-related low mood.

Side effects include headaches and spotting. Some treatments increase blood clot or breast cancer risk.
But decades of fear shouldn't silence today's conversations.

Ask your doctor whether HRT is right for you.
Menopause doesn't mean losing yourself.
