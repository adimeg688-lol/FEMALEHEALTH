Where did I go? Brain fog, changes in mood, sleep, sexual desire and your body can accompany menopause. You deserve to be heard and to understand your options.

HRT can help some menopause symptoms, but it is not right for everyone. Benefits and risks depend on the type of treatment and your medical history. It is not a proven treatment for memory decline or weight loss, and low libido or depression may need separate assessment. Persistent low mood, memory changes or unexpected bleeding deserve clinical attention.

Talk with a qualified healthcare professional about whether HRT is appropriate for you. Educational content, not individual medical advice. AI-generated illustrations are conceptual, not medical diagrams.
