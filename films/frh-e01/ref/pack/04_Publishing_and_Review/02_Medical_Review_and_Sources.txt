CLINICAL REVIEW — BEFORE PUBLISHING
This script is a short educational introduction and is not individualized prescribing guidance. It has not been signed off by a clinician. Retain the approved words unless the reviewer recommends a change. The depicted ribbons and anatomy are symbolic, not a mechanism-of-action diagram. Do not equate the closing flowers with guaranteed recovery.

Claims to verify:
• HRT can relieve vasomotor symptoms, sleep disruption related to menopause and certain menopause-related mood symptoms. Vaginal estrogen is a treatment for vaginal dryness and differs from systemic therapy.
• Headaches and spotting are possible side effects. Unscheduled bleeding can require medical assessment; do not advise viewers to ignore it.
• Some systemic HRT regimens increase risks of blood clots or breast cancer. Risk depends on age, medical history, route, formulation and duration. Avoid implying all preparations carry the same risk.
• Low mood is not the same as diagnosed depression. Persistent or severe depressive symptoms, cognitive concerns, and sexual difficulties merit assessment; HRT is not a proven treatment for cognitive decline or weight loss.
• Historical fears can be contextualized without dismissing genuine risks. Encourage an informed discussion, not unsupervised treatment.
