# FRH · Episode 01 · Where Did I Go?

A woman in menopause no longer recognises herself; she learns what HRT can and cannot do, and that the conversation with her doctor is hers to have.

Built from the approved production pack (`ref/pack/`, v1.0, 19 Nov 2026). The narration, images and creative direction are the client's and are locked.

- **Look:** moving oil painting, assembled from the five supplied paintings (`img/`, 941 × 1672). They are never redrawn or recoloured; motion, light, particles, type and grade are drawn in code over them.
- **Size:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps · **Length:** 45.0 s (the mix's exact length)
- **For:** Instagram Reels / TikTok / Shorts, women in perimenopause or menopause.
- **Sound:** ElevenLabs voice "Amy – Warm, Mature, Audiobook Narrator" (eleven_multilingual_v2), take 01 of 2, placed at 0.4 s, untouched. Score written in code (`score.mjs`): sparse felt piano and soft strings, D minor → F major, no vocals. Mixed to −16 LUFS, music about 20 dB under the voice while she speaks.
- **Captions:** Inter SemiBold 58 px, ivory `#F3E9DB`, the spoken word gold `#F0C98C`, dark outline and soft shadow on a feathered plum backing. One short line at a time, baseline y = 1240 (y = 1440 in s04, to keep clear of the scales). A clean version is rendered without them, plus a corrected two-line SRT.

## The three things a film needs
- **Frame 0:** the fractured mirror with "Where Did I Go?" (Cormorant Garamond) and "MENOPAUSE & HRT". It is the cover.
- **The turn:** 23.05 s, the ribbon of light becomes the beam of the balance: the benefits are said, now the risks.
- **The last image:** the dark plum end card over the last painting, ivory "Menopause doesn't mean losing yourself.", gold FRH / FEMALE REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH, gold motes still drifting (42.2–45 s).
