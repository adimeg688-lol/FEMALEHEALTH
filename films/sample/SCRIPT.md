## The three things a film needs
- **Frame 0:** what is already on screen before anything moves. It is the thumbnail.
- **The turn:** the moment the picture changes meaning, and the second it happens (55–85% of the way in).
- **The last image:** what is held for the final 2–3 seconds.

## Palette
Four to six colours with their hex values, and how the light changes from the first shot to the last.

**Seconds to look at:** every hit, turn and join by its second (the draw at 7.0, the bell at 10.6). They go in each shot's `also` list in `film.js`, so the stills pass shows them.

## Words
Every line as it is spoken, with who says it. About 2.3 words a second for a calm voice, 3 for a fast one. Leave 2–3 s after the last word.
