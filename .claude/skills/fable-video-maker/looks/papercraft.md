# Papercraft

**Use it for:** a silent crafted story of 22–32 s. Music and effects, a 3–7 word hook on frame 0 at most, a turn at 55–85%, a held last image.

## The picture

Layers of cut paper with soft shadows between the sheets.

- Every shape is a sheet: a flat fill with a faint paper fibre, a slightly irregular cut edge, and a soft shadow on the sheet below. Depth comes from the stack, and the shadows grow as a sheet lifts.
- **Palette that has worked:** cream `#F4E8D0`, coral `#E06B5C`, teal `#2A6F7A`, charcoal `#2B2B2B`.
- Things fold and unfold: draw the fold as two sheets hinged on a crease, with the under-side a shade darker.
- Life: sheets that breathe a pixel or two, paper birds, drifting scraps, a light that slides across and moves every shadow.

The story that has worked: a folded crane unfolds, flies over a paper city, and at the turn folds back into a letter that lands in a child's paper hand.

## Camera, captions

Slow push-ins; a gentle slide is allowed because the layers give real parallax. No captions beyond the opening hook, set in the film's own paper (cut letters), inside the clear area.

## Mistakes already made

Photographs of real paper. Printed or typed text inside the picture. Shadows that all point different ways. Shapes with vector-perfect edges.

## To start an unattended run

> Build this silent papercraft story: stacked cut-paper sheets with soft shadows between them, irregular cut edges, folds drawn as hinged sheets. The palette in SCRIPT.md. A hook on frame 0, the turn at the second the script names, a held last image. No printed text in the picture.
