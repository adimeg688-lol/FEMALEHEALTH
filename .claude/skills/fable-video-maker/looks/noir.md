# Noir

**Use it for:** 25–30 s night-city stories: a 1940s city, Art Deco, a huge red moon, one recurring figure. Silent (music and effects) or narrated in the first person.

## The picture

Flat painted shapes with hard edges, in the spirit of 1990s animated noir.

- **Architecture:** stepped skyscrapers, spires, arched windows, fluted columns, sunburst motifs. Bold and geometric.
- **Deep black shadow masses and one dramatic light in every shot.**
- **Palette:** near-black `#07060C`, midnight blue `#10142B`, crimson `#B3121F` and `#5C0A12`, the moon `#E63A2E`, window amber `#FFB347`, cold blue rim `#5C7CFF`, bone-white highlights, now and then a sickly green lamp `#5BE08B`.
- **The red moon** is in at least three shots: a giant disc, a soft glow, faint seas on its face.
- **Layers:** sky, far skyline, middle buildings, foreground silhouettes.
- **Life from inside the frame:** rain, steam, a flickering neon, a cape, smoke, a swinging lamp.
- **Finish:** fine grain, a vignette, bloom on lamps and the moon. **No fake VHS, no scratches, no colour-fringe tricks.**

**The recurring figure.** A series needs one, drawn the same in every film, even as a small appearance. Design your own and write it into the script. The one that has worked: a black hat, a long black cape with a tall collar and a deep-red lining, a trench coat, gloves, the face in shadow under the brim, a thin red rim of light on hat and cape. In a story shot the figure is at least 12% of the frame's height, and is best placed against the moon or a lit window.

## Camera

Only slow eased push-ins (1.00 → 1.04–1.08), hard cuts on the beat, an end hold of about 2.5 s. A silent action film may ask for one whip-pan in its script; otherwise stay on push-ins.

## Captions

Narrated films: bold sans capitals (Poppins Bold or similar), 44 px, letter-spacing about 2 px, bone-white `#F2E9D8`, **the spoken word in amber** `#FFB347`, a thin black outline and a soft shadow, one line of 22 characters or fewer at the house baseline. **Silent films carry no captions.** No lettering in the picture itself.

## Sound

Narrated: a deep, gravelly first-person voice. Music and effects, often written in code (a muted trumpet, a walking bass, brushed snare, rails, rain, the click of cuffs), 8–10 dB under the voice. Silent: the score carries the arc, with a hit on the twist.

## Mistakes already made

Soft mushy silhouettes. A figure smaller than 12% of the frame in a story shot. A film without the figure or without the moon. VHS tricks. Typography in the picture. A twist that cannot be read with the sound off.
