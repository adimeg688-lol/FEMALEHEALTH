# Talking anime

**Use it for:** a 30–60 s short with original characters who talk: voices, lip-sync, a world that feels hand-painted. 46 s is the length that has worked.

## The picture

Hand-painted cel animation: clean shapes, soft gradient shading, **warm ink-brown outlines**, brush grain and paper noise, bloom on lights, rays at dusk, a vignette.

- **Clouds and land are the depth engine:** four to six parallax layers, puffy clouds with lilac shadows and warm tops, something in front that overlaps the subject.
- **A palette arc** across the film: golden dusk, then violet twilight, then moonlit indigo.
- **Characters with faces that read:** real proportions, expressive eyes with highlights, a detail that makes each one theirs (freckles, a silhouette, a spark of magic), hair and cloth in the wind. Three contrasting designs are easier to tell apart than two similar ones: for example a young woman, a wizard with a distinct outline and drifting star-motes, a small fire-spirit with huge eyes and a flickering mouth. **Invent them.** Never copy a character, a creature or a building from a film that exists.
- **A whimsical set piece** earns its place: a walking house on jointed legs (patchwork turrets, copper chimneys, glowing windows, washing on a line, a body that dips at each footfall, a puff of smoke) is the kind of thing this look does well. Design your own.

## Motion

Slow confident drifts and push-ins, holds of several seconds, wipes of cloud or mist between shots. Constant life: parallax, drifting cloud, particles, hair and cloth, blinks, breathing, pots swinging in a kitchen, a small shake and a puff of dust on each heavy step. Tie what we see to what we hear: footsteps, a shooting star, the whoosh of a leap.

**Lip-sync** from `audio/mouth.json`: three mouth shapes (a shut line, a small oval, open with a hint of teeth or tongue), interpolated, shut between words. The speaker is the one moving; the listener blinks and breathes.

## Captions

The house standard with **the spoken word in warm amber** `#ffb347`, a 0.12 s fade, at the house baseline (y = 1240). Compose the shots so faces sit above the caption line; do not drop the captions into the bottom of the frame to make room.

## Sound

Two or three contrasting ElevenLabs voices (`references/audio.md`), a light score (a waltz has worked), and effects: footsteps, fire, wind, a sparkle, a whoosh.

## Mistakes already made

Copying a known wizard, witch or castle. Flat paper-doll faces. Missing lip-sync. An empty sky with one layer of cloud. No change of palette from dusk to night. Running the full capture before the stills were good. Captions on faces.

## To start an unattended run

> Build an original painterly anime short: characters with readable faces who talk, lip-sync from audio/mouth.json, blinks, hair and cloth in the wind, four to six parallax layers of cloud and land, a palette that runs from dusk to night. Do not copy any character or building from an existing film. Captions from audio/captions.json with the spoken word in amber. The audio is final.
