# Lamp loop

**Use it for:** short, relatable life-lesson and comparison gags. The pattern that travels furthest is a **12–14 s loop, music only, one locked scene, and a label that flips on the punchline.** A narrated essay of about 40 s is the exception.

## The picture

- **Near-black** (`#050505` to `#111`) with one or two warm lamps, hanging or in the street: a soft cone of light, a glowing pool on the floor, a dark tiled floor with a faint reflection.
- Almost monochrome plus **one red accent** `#E0262B` on the object the gag is about.
- **Faceless figures:** a plain circle for a head with a thick black outline and no face; thick-limbed white stick figures or slightly fuller mannequins. The hero is bright white with a soft glow; the others are grey. All the feeling is in the pose, the props and the labels.
- Flat shapes, soft radial light, a vignette, drop shadows, a few specks of dust, reflections on the floor.

## Type

Small capitals in a squared technical face (Oxanium SemiBold, or Share Tech Mono, Chakra Petch), about 34 px with wide letter-spacing. **Top centre, 13–17% down the frame**, one or two lines, usually held for the whole film. Labels sit above the figures ("ME" / "MONDAY") and can swap on the punchline. A key word may be red. A tiny counter or clock is allowed. **No watermark, no page name, no call to action on screen**: the ask belongs in the post's caption.

Captions do not sit at 65% in this look. They are the lines at the top.

## Camera

One locked shot, with at most a 3–5% slow push. **The first and the last frame match, so it loops.** A 12-second gag almost never has a cut. A narrated essay uses one small scene a line with quick crossfades.

## Sound

Music that fits the feeling, with a clear hit on the punchline: hard and driving for gags, a slowed piano or an ambient bed for the tender ones, lo-fi for daily life. Narrated: a calm, mid-low voice over a ducked piano.

## Mistakes already made

Drawn faces. A busy camera. Captions at the 65% line. A watermark. A call to action on screen. A last frame that does not match the first.

## To start an unattended run

> Build a 12–14 second loop: faceless circle-head figures in a black lamp-lit scene, one red accent, small spaced capitals at the top (about 15% down), one locked camera, a label that flips on the punchline. The first and last frames match. No watermark. Music with a hit on the punchline.
