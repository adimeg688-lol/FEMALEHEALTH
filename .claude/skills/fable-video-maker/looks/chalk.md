# Chalk

**Use it for:** a silent crafted story of 22–32 s on a blackboard. Music and effects, a turn at 55–85%, a held last image.

## The picture

- **The board:** black with dusty smudges, old erased marks, a little grain. Baked once.
- **The line:** imperfect chalk strokes with a broken, powdery edge and uneven pressure. Lines are drawn on, stroke by stroke, and dust falls from them.
- **Pastel chalks:** pink `#F2A0B8`, mint `#9EE0C5`, yellow `#F5D76E`, and white.
- Life: dust drifting down, a line that redraws itself slightly every few frames (seeded by the frame number), smudges that spread where something passed.

The story that has worked: a chalk circle becomes a door onto a field of stars; at the turn the dust settles into two words, "miss you".

## Camera, captions

Slow push-ins. No captions. The only words are the ones the story itself draws in dust, and they are the exception that proves the rule.

## Mistakes already made

Clean vector shapes. Neat typography in the drawing. Lines with a perfect edge. A board that is plain black.

## To start an unattended run

> Build this silent chalk story: a dusty blackboard, imperfect powdery strokes drawn on one at a time, pastel chalks from SCRIPT.md, dust that falls and settles. A hook on frame 0, the turn at the second the script names, a held last image. No typography except the words the dust forms.
