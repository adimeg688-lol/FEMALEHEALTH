# Painted

**Use it for:** quiet, sincere films: scripture, sermons, a journey of the soul, landscapes. Music only, a spoken reflection, or a real talk the person may use, re-told as pictures. 20–80 s; most land between 20 and 50.

## The picture

A moving impressionist oil painting. Not a filtered photo, and not flat colour with an "oil" texture on top.

- **A layered 2.5D matte:** the sky with painted clouds (noise warped by a flow field), two to four planes of hills, forest or mountain in aerial perspective, a middle ground, and a foreground of large dark silhouettes (grass, branches, rocks) slightly soft. Four to seven planes in all.
- **A small faceless figure** in a pose that reads: standing, walking, arms open, sitting, looking up. Tiny in a vast landscape.
- **Light is the hero.** A low golden sun, rays through haze, rim light, long shadows, light bouncing up from the ground.
- **Brushwork that follows form.** Strokes run along hills, around trunks, across water. Thicker paint on the highlights. Canvas weave at 6–8%. A warm vintage grade: teal in the shadows, amber in the lights, lifted blacks, a gentle vignette.
- **Paint boil.** Re-seed the strokes' displacement every second or third frame (10–12 paint frames a second), by a few pixels, only inside the strokes. The composition and the camera stay perfectly smooth. Seed it from the frame number so any second can be drawn alone.
- **Things sit in the land.** A cart, an ox, a tree has a contact shadow, is overlapped by grass, and takes the same light as the ground under it.
- **Life in every shot:** leaves, birds, visible rain and drips, chaff, falling autumn leaves, fireflies, drifting pollen, cloth.

The palette changes with the film (dusk orange-pink and violet; misty green fields; forest gold shafts; night blue with one warm lamp; desert ochre; sunrise gold) under **one shared grade**, so it reads as one painter.

A city of light at the end of a film is a radiant, detailed city in the clouds (towers, domes, arches, glowing windows). Never a row of yellow rectangles.

## For scripture and sermon films

A timeless world: no cars, phones, power lines, modern buildings or readable signs. People are faceless painted silhouettes. If a Christ figure appears at all, it is a pale robe seen from behind or a luminous silhouette, always small: no face, no halo, no actor's likeness, no glowing hands. Respectful and sincere, never kitsch. If the source is someone talking to a camera, you re-tell the ideas as scenes; you do not draw the speaker.

## Camera

Only slow eased push-ins or pull-outs (1.00 ↔ 1.06), or a very slow vertical drift. Shots of 2.5 s or more, joined by 8–12 frame crossfades on the beat. A still end hold of 2.5–3.8 s, alive until its last frame.

## Captions

The house standard: Lora, warm white, the spoken word gold, one short line at 65% of the height. A music-only film may carry a few short lines, or none.

## Sound

Usually provided: a song, a talk, or a spoken scene over a new instrumental bed. Mux what you are given. Do not write a replacement score unless asked.

## Mistakes already made

One oil texture smeared over flat gradients. Clip-art animals and carts that float on the field. Trees as vertical bars. Middle-of-the-film landscapes that go soft and empty: look hardest at those. A last shot frozen for six seconds. Sideways camera sway. Long two-line captions. Modern props. Faces that look like an actor.

## To start an unattended run

> Build this painted film: layered 2.5D mattes, brushwork that follows form, paint boil, faceless figures, no modern objects. Camera: only slow eased push-ins. House captions: Lora 600, warm white #fff1d6, the spoken word gold #ffc94d, one line of 22 characters or fewer, baseline at 65% of the height, nothing in the bottom 22%. The audio is final.
