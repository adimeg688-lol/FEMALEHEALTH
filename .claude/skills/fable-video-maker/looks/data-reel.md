# Data reel

**Use it for:** calm, credible product and data explainers for founders and marketers. 30–90 s. The tone is evidence, not excitement.

## The picture

A dark product dashboard and moving type. No characters, no papercraft, no chalk.

- **Palette that has worked:** ground `#070B12`, panel `#0E1622`, panel line `#1C2A3A`, text `#E8F1F8`, muted `#7A8CA0`, one accent (cyan `#22D3EE`), a green for "live" `#34D399`, an amber for warnings `#F5B841`, used sparingly. With a brand's own palette, use theirs exactly.
- **Big tabular numbers** (Anton or Archivo Black) and small labels (Inter). Rounded cards (about 18 px), 1 px borders, a soft glow on the hero number, light grain, a slight vignette.
- **Motion:** counters that roll, cards that slide in with a small overshoot, a playhead crossing a timeline, parallax across a board of tiles. A new picture every 1.5–3 s.

## What it may show

- **Every number on screen comes from the source the person gave you.** If a number is not in it, it is not in the film. Put the source table in `SCRIPT.md`.
- No hype words, no emojis on screen, no invented money.
- No other company's name or logo, no readable packaging, no photographs of someone else's product. Anonymise: a generic silhouette, "a supplement brand", a day counter.
- Money figures (spend, cost, return) only when the person supplies and approves them.
- **Remove every placeholder and test label before the 1080 render.** A chip left over from a preview has reached a final before. Search the code for them.

## Camera, captions

Slow push-ins inside a shot; cuts and slides between them. Captions of 2–4 words with the spoken word lit, at the house baseline, **never over the numbers**: keep the key interface between y = 300 and y = 1150. End card: the brand's wordmark and one short ask.

Use `--png` for the capture: flat dark panels band in JPEG.

## Sound

A supplied voice, untouched, with its word times. A quiet bed under it.
