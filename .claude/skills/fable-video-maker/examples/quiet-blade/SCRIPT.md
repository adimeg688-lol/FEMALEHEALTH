# THE QUIET BLADE

A young swordsman climbs to challenge an old master who will not even turn round; he draws, nothing answers him but falling petals, and he sits down beside the man he came to fight.

- **The turn:** 10.6 s (66%). The blade is at the master's back, the master has not moved, petals pour through the frame, one settles on the steel, and the point sinks to the ground.
